FOR SOUTH AFRICAN IMPORTERS

SARS documentation requirements

Since April 2025, SARS requires invoice data — including Incoterms and payment terms — to be captured at declaration stage. Declarations without compliant invoice data face a higher probability of inspection and audit. Meridian prepares commercial documentation to meet these requirements before goods leave China.

NRCS and ICASA pre-clearance

Electronics and hardware imported into South Africa require NRCS Letters of Authority and, where RF or telecoms equipment is involved, ICASA type approval — both secured before customs release. Meridian advises on applicable compulsory specifications and assists with documentation for the pre-clearance process.

Supplier verification from within China

Ghost factories, bait-and-switch quality, and misrepresented export credentials are documented risks for SA importers sourcing from China without on-the-ground representation. Meridian verifies suppliers through China's National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System and established Pearl River Delta networks before any commercial engagement.
