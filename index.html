01How China orders fail

Three failures, found before money moves

The supplier

The supplier calls itself a factory, but its licence shows a trading company, or the contract, invoice and bank account name three different companies. You find out after the deposit. The Supplier Check compares them first.

The product

The test report covers a similar model, or comes from a laboratory the NRCS does not accept. Mains-powered goods need an NRCS Letter of Authority, and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth devices need ICASA approval. The Import Compliance Assessment names the rules and the documents to request.

The border

Goods are held because they do not comply, or because the importer on the shipping documents is not the Letter of Authority holder. Non-compliant goods can be returned or destroyed. The Pre-Order Check matches these names before you order.

03Regulatory updates

Rule changes that affect importers from China

Dated notices, newest first.

30 September 2026. South Africa

South Africa’s PVoC programme is on hold

The Pre-Export Verification of Conformity (PVoC) programme was gazetted on 20 March 2026 for listed goods from China. In June 2026 the SABS put implementation on hold pending consultation. We have found no announcement that it has resumed.

The Gazette applies it only to products outside NRCS compulsory specifications. NRCS-regulated electrical goods still need a Letter of Authority.

Reference: Government Gazette No. 54374. Last checked 30 September 2026.
