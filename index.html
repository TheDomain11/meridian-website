Where China imports go wrong

Most losses happen before the goods ship

The expensive mistakes are rarely about price. They come from paying the wrong party, signing terms that no court in China will enforce, or shipping goods your own border will not accept.

The deposit

A deposit goes to a "factory" that turns out to be a trading company, or a business whose registration no longer matches the name on the invoice.

The terms

An English-only contract under foreign law, with no quality annex and no independent inspection before the balance is paid. When the goods arrive wrong, there is little to enforce.

The border

Electrical goods arrive without an NRCS Letter of Authority, or the invoice, packing list and tariff code do not agree, and the shipment is held while SARS asks questions.
